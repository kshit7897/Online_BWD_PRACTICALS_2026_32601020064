Our Story

The Faculty of Computer Applications (FCA) at Marwadi University was started with a goal to provide quality IT education to students of Saurashtra and across India. Today the faculty offers BCA, MCA and Ph.D. programs with a strong focus on practical learning, projects and industry exposure.

Our students regularly take part in national level hackathons, publish research papers and get placed in leading IT companies.

Vision

To be a centre of excellence in computer education producing skilled, ethical and innovative IT professionals.

Mission

  • Provide quality education with modern teaching methods.
  • Develop practical skills through hands-on projects.
  • Encourage research, innovation and entrepreneurship.
