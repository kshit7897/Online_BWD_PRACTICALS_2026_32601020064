Faculty of Computer Applications (FCA)

Marwadi University, Rajkot

Our Vision

To shape confident, skilled and responsible IT professionals through quality computer education, innovation and strong values.

Our Mission

  1. To deliver up-to-date computer education using modern teaching tools.
  2. To build practical skills through lab work, projects and internships.
  3. To motivate students towards research, start-ups and innovation.
  4. To connect students with industry for training and placements.
  5. To develop discipline, ethics and social responsibility.

Programs Offered

  • Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA)
  • BCA – Online
  • Master of Computer Applications (MCA)
  • Ph.D. in Computer Applications
