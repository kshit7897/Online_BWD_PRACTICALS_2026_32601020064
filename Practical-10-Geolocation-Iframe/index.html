Purpose of Using an iframe

  • An <iframe> (inline frame) is used to embed another webpage inside the current webpage.
  • Here it is used to show a Google Map of the college, so visitors can see the location without leaving our site.
  • It can also embed YouTube videos, forms, documents or other websites.
  • Important attributes: src, width, height, title, loading="lazy", allowfullscreen.
  • Content inside the iframe is loaded separately, so it does not affect our page's code.
