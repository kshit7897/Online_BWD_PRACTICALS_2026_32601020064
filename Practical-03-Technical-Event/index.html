Event Details

Tech Fest 2026 is a one-day technical event where students showcase their skills in coding, robotics, AI and web development. Winners get trophies, certificates and internship offers from partner companies.

  • Competitions: Coding Contest, Web Design, Robotics, AI Quiz
  • Team Size: 1 – 3 members
  • Registration Fee: ₹150 per participant
  • Prizes: ₹21,000 (1st), ₹11,000 (2nd), ₹5,100 (3rd)
  • Eligibility: All BCA / MCA / B.Tech / B.Sc. IT students

Venue, Date & Time

Venue: College Auditorium, Marwadi University, Rajkot
Date: 20th November 2026
Time: 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM
